TL;DR: 本文通过风洞模拟实验研究了星系穿越星系团内介质（ICM）密度和温度不连续区域（如晃动螺旋结构）时的影响。结果显示，星系在穿越高密度不连续区域时气体损失显著增加，但恒星形成速率和(u-i)颜色指数的变化幅度有限（最多5%），且效应短暂，最终会恢复到与对照模型相似的状态。

Motivation: 星系团碰撞合并会产生密度和温度不连续的区域（如晃动螺旋结构），此类环境可能影响星系演化。作者旨在探究穿越这些不连续区域的星系如何受到物理影响。

Method: 采用风洞模拟装置，将类银河系质量星系插入其中。构建了8个模型：2个对照模型和6个包含不同密度/温度过渡的模型（涵盖低密度和高密度环境），模拟星系穿越ICM不连续区域的过程。

Result: 穿越高密度不连续区域时，星系气体损失显著增加；恒星形成率短暂增强，颜色指数轻微变蓝；但变化幅度不超过5%，且最终恢复到对照模型水平。

Conclusion: 穿越ICM密度和温度不连续区域对星系产生可测量但微弱且短暂的影响，不足以导致星系特性的持久改变。

Abstract: Cluster collisions and mergers are among the most energetic phenomena in the low redshift Universe. These interactions disturb the intracluster medium creating regions with density and temperature discontinuities, such as sloshing spirals. There is evidence that such environments can influence galaxy evolution. This study aims to understand how a galaxy that crosses an environment with discontinuities in density and temperature can be affected. To this end, a set of simulations was conducted using a wind tunnel setup into which a {MW-like mass} galaxy was inserted. A total of eight models were created, namely two control runs and six with distinct density and temperature transitions along the tunnel, comprising simulations with low density and high density environments. Results show that galaxies lose {considerably} more gas due to the higher density encountered when crossing denser and discontinuous regions in comparison to a constant density environment. The star formation rate exhibits a brief enhancement when the galaxy enters the denser section of the tunnel and {(u-i)} color index also undergoes slight changes, initially becoming bluer. However, even in the simulations with the most intense transitions, changes {in star formation rate and color index} are not substantial, reaching at most 5\% difference in relation to the control models. By the end of the simulation runs, star formation rate and color index are similar to the control runs. These results suggest that crossing intracluster medium discontinuities can induce measurable effects in a galaxy, but these are subtle and short-lived.
