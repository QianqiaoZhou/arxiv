TL;DR: 利用XMM-Newton观测，在星系团A2142中探测到一个马赫数约1.3的并合激波，并与四个冷锋共同被数值模拟再现。该激波与头尾射电星系T1/T2背后的弧状射电纤维空间重合，被解释为激波与射电星系低密度荚膜相互作用产生的部分涡环结构（类似“烟圈”）。激波还可通过再加速老化相对论电子和拉伸尾等离子体显著拉长射电尾，为超过500 kpc的射电尾提供自然解释。研究确立了射电弧和超长射电尾作为并合激波独立互补示踪物的角色，表明并合激波能重塑星系团的热和非热成分，尾射电星系是灵敏的星系团内介质天气探针。

Motivation: 研究大质量并合星系团A2142中的激波与射电结构的相互作用机制，揭示并合激波如何同时影响星系团的热气体（X射线）和非热成分（射电），并探索射电弧和超长射电尾作为并合激波示踪物的可行性。

Method: 使用XMM-Newton X射线观测数据探测并合激波并测量马赫数；利用数值模拟（大碰撞参数的偏心并合模拟）重现激波和冷锋结构；结合射电观测识别激波与射电星系尾部的空间关联；参照磁流体动力学模拟结果解释射电弧的涡环结构形成机制。

Result: 在A2142西北侧探测到马赫数M~1.3的并合激波；激波与四个冷锋可通过大碰撞参数的偏心并合模拟再现；激波与头尾射电星系T1/T2后的弧状射电纤维空间重合；激波通过剥离和卷曲射电星系荚膜形成类似涡环（烟圈）的射电弧；激波还能再加速老化电子并拉伸尾等离子体，产生超过500 kpc的超长射电尾。

Conclusion: 射电弧和超长射电尾可作为并合激波的独立且互补的示踪物；并合激波能同时重塑星系团的热气体和非热射电成分；尾射电星系是灵敏的星系团内介质（ICM）天气探针。

Abstract: Abell 2142 (A2142) is a massive, nearby galaxy cluster undergoing a complex merger. It exhibits an elongated X-ray morphology along the northwest-southeast axis and hosts four known cold fronts. Using XMM-Newton observations, we detect a merger shock on the northwest side of the cluster with a Mach number of $M \sim 1.3$. The observed shock front and four cold fronts can be reproduced by numerical simulations of an off-axis merger with a large impact parameter, which imparts significant angular momentum to induce the sloshing of the subcluster core and large-scale ambient gas. In projection, the shock front is spatially coincident with arc-shaped radio filaments observed behind the prominent head-tail radio galaxies T1 and T2. We interpret these radio arcs as partial vortex ring structures (resembling ``smoke rings'') produced by the interaction of the merger shock with the low-density cocoons of radio galaxies. The shock strips and rolls the jet cocoon into a toroidal vortex, as predicted by recent magnetohydrodynamic simulations. We further demonstrate that the merger shock can significantly elongate the radio tails by re-accelerating aged relativistic electrons and stretching the tail plasma via the post-shock wind. This process provides a natural explanation for the $>$500 kpc tail observed in this and other merging clusters. Our findings establish radio arcs and ultra-long radio tails as independent, complementary tracers of merger shocks in galaxy clusters. Our results demonstrate that merger shocks can reshape both the thermal and non-thermal components of galaxy clusters, and that tailed radio galaxies serve as sensitive probes of intracluster medium weather.
